# Online Retail Store Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes online retail sales data using Power BI to generate business insights related to revenue trends, customer behavior, international demand, and expansion opportunities.

## Key Insights
- Total Revenue Analyzed: £8.34M+
- Top 10 Customers contributed approximately 17% of total revenue
- International markets generated 912K+ units sold outside the UK
- Netherlands emerged as the highest-demand international market
- Strong seasonal growth observed during Q4, especially in November 2011

## Dashboards Created
1. Monthly Revenue Trend Analysis
2. Country-wise Revenue & Quantity Analysis
3. Top 10 Customer Revenue Analysis
4. Global Product Demand Analysis

# Business Problem

The CEO and CMO of the online retail company wanted to understand the overall business performance, customer behavior, international market opportunities, and revenue trends using transactional sales data.

The primary business objectives were:

- Analyze monthly revenue trends for forecasting and seasonal demand analysis
- Identify top-performing international markets outside the United Kingdom
- Understand customer revenue contribution and retention opportunities
- Visualize global product demand for international expansion planning

The dashboard was developed to transform raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights that support executive decision-making.

---

# Walkthrough of Key Visuals

## 1. Monthly Revenue Trend Analysis

A line chart was created to analyze monthly revenue performance for the year 2011.

### Key Insights:
- Strong revenue growth was observed during Q4
- November recorded the highest monthly revenue
- Seasonal purchasing behavior was clearly visible
- Revenue decline in December may indicate incomplete transactional data

### Business Value:
Helps leadership improve forecasting, inventory planning, and seasonal marketing strategies.

---

## 2. Country-wise Revenue & Quantity Analysis

A clustered bar chart was created to compare revenue and quantity sold across the top 10 countries excluding the United Kingdom.

### Key Insights:
- Netherlands, EIRE, Germany, and France emerged as strong international markets
- Some countries generated high quantities but comparatively lower revenue
- International sales performance varied significantly across regions

### Business Value:
Supports expansion planning and region-specific marketing and pricing strategies.

---

## 3. Top 10 Customers by Revenue

A customer analysis dashboard was created to identify the top revenue-generating customers.

### Key Insights:
- Top 10 customers contributed approximately 17% of total revenue
- High-value customers remain strategically important
- Revenue generation is relatively diversified across the customer base

### Business Value:
Supports customer retention strategies, loyalty programs, and personalized engagement initiatives.

---

## 4. Global Product Demand Analysis

A map visualization was created to analyze international product demand by country excluding the United Kingdom.

### Key Insights:
- International demand was strongest across European countries
- Netherlands showed the highest quantity sold internationally
- Several countries demonstrated strong customer engagement

### Business Value:
Helps identify geographic expansion opportunities and high-performing international markets.
